THE IMPORTANT BITS

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is profit divided by cost. Margin is profit divided by selling price (excluding VAT). If a job costs £100 and you charge £130, markup is 30%, but margin is about 23.1%.

Is this tool genuinely free?

Yes. Building, saving and printing a customer estimate is free and requires no login. The optional £9.99 Premium Workbook bundle below also includes a PRO browser app.

Where are my quotes saved?

Only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Your data could disappear if you clear browser storage or use private browsing. Use “Download backup” for a portable copy.

Can I use this as an official invoice or tax calculation?

No. This is a costing and estimate tool, not accounting software, a tax return, an invoice system or legal advice. Confirm the correct VAT and any other tax obligations with HMRC or a qualified professional.
