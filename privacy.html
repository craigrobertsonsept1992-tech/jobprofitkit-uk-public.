Free calculator

The free calculator stores your quote information only in your browser using local storage. Our website does not upload quote data, customer names or job amounts to our servers. You can delete the information by clearing your browser site storage or using the reset control.

Hosting

Customers using the separately delivered PRO HTML app store business and customer records locally in their own browser. They should secure their device and backups; we do not receive this data unless they deliberately email or share it with us.
